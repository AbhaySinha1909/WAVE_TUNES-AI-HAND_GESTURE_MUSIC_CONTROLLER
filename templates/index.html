How to Use

Follow these simple steps to enjoy hands-free music control!

  • 🖐 Open palm → Play
  • 👉 Swipe right → Next track
  • 👈 Swipe left → Previous track
  • ✊ Fist → Pause
  • 👍 Thumbs_up → Volume Up
  • 👎 Thumbs_Down → Volume Down
  • 👌 Okay → Mute/Unmute

Make sure your camera is enabled before starting!
